# Supplyico

Supplyico is an AI-powered sourcing agent for e-commerce brands. We find verified suppliers at 20-40% less than brands are currently paying.

## How it works

1. We run a free sourcing audit on one of your products — your current cost vs what we find from verified manufacturers
2. If the savings are real, we handle ongoing sourcing — finding suppliers, negotiating prices, managing quality
3. You only pay 25% of verified savings. No savings = no fee.

## What we source

Any physical product sold by e-commerce brands: apparel, accessories, cosmetics, skincare, homeware, candles, packaging, phone cases, bags, jewellery, supplements, pet products.

## Why brands switch

- Most brands pick a supplier once and never compare prices again
- We search manufacturer networks that small brands can't access on their own
- Zero risk: free audit, performance-based pricing, no lock-in

## Pricing

- Free sourcing audit on one product (the hook)
- 25% of verified savings (performance-based)
- Optional €500 onboarding fee for full catalogue sourcing
