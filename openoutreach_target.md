# Target market

Small and mid-sized e-commerce brands in Ireland and the UK doing €5,000 to €50,000 per month in revenue.

## Who we're looking for

- Founders or ops managers at DTC (direct-to-consumer) e-commerce brands
- Brands selling physical products (not digital, not services)
- Brands on Shopify, WooCommerce, or selling via their own website
- Ideally in: fashion, streetwear, beauty, skincare, homeware, candles, accessories, food & drink packaging
- Based in Ireland or the UK (we can source for EU brands too)

## Ideal customer profile

- Revenue: €5k-50k/month
- Team size: 1-15 people
- Currently sourcing from 1-3 suppliers
- Has never done a formal sourcing comparison or audit
- Founder handles sourcing alongside everything else

## Not a fit

- Brands doing under €2k/month (not enough volume for meaningful savings)
- Brands with a dedicated procurement team and existing manufacturer relationships
- Pure dropshipping businesses (no inventory, no sourcing need)
- Digital-only products
